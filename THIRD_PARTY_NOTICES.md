# Third-party notices

## KU100 measured responses

**Spherical Near-Field HRTF Set — circular KU100 measurements**, Johannes M. Arend, Annika Neidhardt and Christoph Pörschmann, TH Köln. Source: [Zenodo 4297951](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4297951). Source-file license: [CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Preparation sorts directions, applies the authors' distance-gain correction once to both ears, converts complete coefficients to Float32 and writes the documented bank. It does not normalize, trim, equalize or resample the responses. [data/LICENSE_DATA.md](data/LICENSE_DATA.md) records the payload/record license discrepancy, attribution and modifications; [data/manifest.json](data/manifest.json) records exact hashes.

The bank and measured-response derivatives, including the airborne demonstration WAV and its embedded bundle, retain CC BY-SA 3.0. This is separate from the MIT license of original software. The viewer identifies creators, source, license and modifications. No endorsement is implied.

**SADIE II Database**, AudioLab, University of York; Gavin Kearney, Callum Armstrong and Lewis Thresh.
