What to listen for

Same sound family? Is this recognizably the close wet texture you requested, or another unrelated sound?

Structure? Compare attacks, clustered bursts, quiet gaps and evolving texture—not just brightness.

Both ears? Compare closeness, near/far detail and separation. The quieter ear must not disappear.

Action correspondence? Leave unjudged when motion is not observed. Do not infer peeling, suction or bubbles from a click alone.
