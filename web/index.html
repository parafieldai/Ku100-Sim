Know what is modeled

EVIDENCE & LIMITS
MEASURED

KU100 airborne transfer shape

The airborne mode uses measured directional transfer shape and relative distance scaling. Absolute airborne pressure is uncalibrated. This bank does not calibrate contact, materials, or internal head transmission.

Near-field KU100 dataset ↗
GENERIC

Contact mechanics & geometry

The fixture and its contact patch are model geometry. Treat predicted local pressure and energy as solver outputs, with calibration and convergence limits described by each export.

CAPTURE CHAIN

Load a render to inspect its declared sensitivity, electronic gain, and ADC scale.

COMPARE

KU100 ≠ 3Dio

KU100 uses a head enclosure; 3Dio uses an ear-and-disc assembly. A KU100 transfer bank cannot establish a 3Dio response. Comparison needs receiver-specific measurements and the same physical excitation.
