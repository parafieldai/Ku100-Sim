How the longer sound was made

The first two right takes and one additional fresh right take are combined at 0, 9 and 18 seconds, using 3-second equal-power overlaps. Nothing is looped or time-stretched. Overlapping independent textures can blend or interrupt individual events; continuity of physical contact is not claimed.

The short WAVs include approximately 21 milliseconds of the existing stereo filter's tail. The 30-second assembly contains the two right takes also provided separately here, so those files are not independent test conditions.
