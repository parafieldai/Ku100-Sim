# Measured HRIR data attribution and license provenance

The measured HRIR data and the derived `ku100_bank.bin` are separate from this project's original code.

**Work:** Spherical Near-Field HRTF Set, circular KU100 measurements.

**Creators:** Johannes M. Arend, Annika Neidhardt, Christoph Pörschmann. Measurement organization: Technische Hochschule Köln, Germany. The SOFA files name Johannes M. Arend as author and provide `Johannes.Arend@th-koeln.de` as contact.

**Source:** [Zenodo record 4297951, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.4297951](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4297951); [original project page](https://audiogroup.web.th-koeln.de/ku100nfhrir.html). Full source URLs and cryptographic hashes are in [manifest.json](manifest.json).

**Research citation:** Arend, J. M., Neidhardt, A., and Pörschmann, C. (2016). *Measurement and Perceptual Evaluation of a Spherical Near-Field HRTF Set*. Proceedings of the 29th VDT International Convention, pp. 356–363.

Preserve attribution, the license reference, and notice of modifications when distributing this derivative. Its license is not replaced by the license of the surrounding source code. No endorsement by the authors or Neumann is claimed.

**Documented discrepancy:** the Zenodo record-level metadata says CC BY 4.0, while its SOFA payloads say CC 3.0 BY-SA. We preserve both facts and retain the payload's share-alike notice for the derivative. We have not obtained a clarification or silently treated the record metadata as permission to remove share-alike.

**Changes:** integer-azimuth ordering, application of the authors' October 2020 distance-gain correction once to both ears, conversion of coefficients to float32, and serialization into the documented native bank. No extra windowing, normalization, per-ear gain, response truncation, EQ, or resampling is added. Validation metadata is generated by this project. Original author PDFs and MAT/SOFA archives are not redistributed in this repository.

The source data measure airborne acoustic transfer. Neither the data license nor the measured response supports a claim of a calibrated contact model or manufacturer-certified digital twin.
